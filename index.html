03 / Approach

Find the source.

Pain relief matters. But if the reason it started is still there, it tends to come back. Every visit starts with a close look at how you move, so treatment goes after the real cause instead of just the symptom.

You'll finish knowing what's going on and why. Understanding your body is what lets you take part in fixing it.

  • Spinal manipulation Spine
  • Extremity adjusting Shoulder · hip · knee · ankle
  • Soft tissue therapy Muscle · fascia
  • Electro-modalities Pain · healing
  • Exercise & movement retraining Long term
  • Lifestyle & nutrition advice Everyday

04 / About

Kinesiologist. Chiropractor. Massage trained.

Paige earned an Honours BSc in Kinesiology and an MSc at the University of Waterloo. She then graduated as a Doctor of Chiropractic from the Canadian Memorial Chiropractic College in 2022. She also completed the Advanced Standing Massage Therapy Diploma at the Royal Canadian College of Massage Therapy.

She's an evidence-based chiropractor who treats a wide range of problems in the spine, joints and muscles, and their effects on the nervous system. Her focus is biomechanical assessment and movement retraining built around each person. The goal isn't just getting you out of pain. It's keeping the pain from coming back.
