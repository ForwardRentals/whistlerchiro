The approach

Treat the cause, not just the symptom.

Pain relief matters. But if the reason it started is still there, it tends to come back. Every visit starts with a careful look at how you move, so treatment goes after the real source of the problem.

You'll leave knowing what's going on and why. When you understand your body, you can take an active role in getting better and staying that way.

Meet Dr. Nosal

Kinesiologist, chiropractor and massage therapist.

Dr. Paige Nosal earned an Honours BSc in Kinesiology and an MSc at the University of Waterloo. She then graduated as a Doctor of Chiropractic from the Canadian Memorial Chiropractic College in 2022. She also completed the Advanced Standing Massage Therapy Diploma at the Royal Canadian College of Massage Therapy.

She is a licensed, evidence-based chiropractor who diagnoses and treats a wide range of neuromusculoskeletal problems. That includes conditions of the spine and extremities and their effects on the nervous system. Her focus is biomechanical assessment and movement retraining tailored to each person.

The goal is more than getting you pain-free. It's keeping the pain from coming back, improving how you function, and helping you live better. Dr. Nosal believes understanding why pain is happening is what empowers people to change it.

Treatments

Hands-on care, built around how you move.

Your plan depends on your assessment. Treatment may include any combination of the following.

Spinal manipulative therapy

Precise adjustments to restore motion in stiff or painful areas of the spine.

Extremity adjusting

Care for shoulders, hips, knees, ankles, wrists and other joints beyond the spine.

Soft tissue therapy

Massage-trained hands that address tight muscles, fascia and trigger points.

Electro-modalities

Therapeutic tools that help manage pain and support tissue healing.

Exercise & movement retraining

Personalised exercise prescription that corrects the movement patterns behind your pain.

Lifestyle & nutrition advice

Practical guidance on the everyday habits that affect recovery and long-term health.
